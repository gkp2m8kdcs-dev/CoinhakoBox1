Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026
Short version: CoinhakoBox stores all your investment data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. No account is required to use the app.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how CoinhakoBox ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with your use of the CoinhakoBox mobile application (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CoinhakoBox is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address).
  • Financial account credentials or brokerage login information.
  • Investment positions, holdings, or trade history.
  • Location data or device identifiers for tracking purposes.
  • Usage analytics tied to your identity.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter into CoinhakoBox — including your watchlist, holdings, trades, notes, and attached photos of brokerage confirmations — is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your iPhone unless you explicitly export it.

3.1 Local Storage

Your portfolio, trade journal, and application settings are saved to the app's secure local sandbox provided by iOS. We do not have remote access to this data.

3.2 Photos and Attachments

If you attach photos to trade records, those images are stored locally on your device within the app's protected storage. They are not uploaded to any server.

4. Information We May Receive

The only limited information we may receive is:

  • Crash diagnostics: If you opt in to share crash and analytics data with Apple, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized crash reports. These reports contain no personal or financial data.
  • Support communications: If you voluntarily contact us by email at karlawsokunevajob68@gmail.com, we will receive whatever information you choose to include in your message.

5. Third-Party Services

CoinhakoBox does not integrate third-party advertising networks, social media trackers, or marketing analytics. The app does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

6. Data Export and Portability

You may export your trades and holdings as CSV files at any time using the in-app export feature. These files are produced and saved by you, and we never see or receive their contents.

7. Children's Privacy

CoinhakoBox is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address the situation.

8. Data Security

We rely on Apple's iOS platform security to protect your locally stored data. Your data is protected by the same iOS sandboxing and encryption that protects other apps on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including the use of a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or modify on our servers. You can, at any time:

  • Delete individual trades or holdings within the app.
  • Export all your data as CSV.
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all locally stored data.

10. International Users

CoinhakoBox is a globally available application. Because all data is stored locally on your device and not transferred to us, your data remains under your control regardless of where you are located.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after a change indicates your acceptance of the updated Policy.
