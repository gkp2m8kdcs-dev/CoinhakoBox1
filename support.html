Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a stock to my watchlist?

Open the Markets tab, search for a ticker, then tap the star icon to add it to your watchlist. You can also tap any stock in search results to view its full detail page.

How do I log a new trade?

Go to the Trade tab and tap the "+" button. Enter the buy or sell details, including symbol, quantity, price, fees, date, and notes. You can also attach a photo of your brokerage confirmation.

Where is my data stored?

All your data — watchlist, holdings, trades, notes, and attached photos — is stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Open the Tools section and choose "Data Export." You can export your trades and holdings as CSV files that you can save, share, or back up however you prefer.

Does CoinhakoBox connect to my brokerage?

No. CoinhakoBox is a tracking and analytics tool. It does not connect to any brokerage, place trades, or hold your funds. You enter trades manually after executing them with your broker.

Which currencies are supported?

The app supports 11 major currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY, HKD, AUD, CAD, CHF, SGD, and KRW. You can set your preferred display currency in Settings.

How do I delete the app and all its data?

To permanently remove all data, simply uninstall CoinhakoBox from your device. Because all data is local, uninstalling will erase everything.

Is the price data real-time?

CoinhakoBox displays market data that updates during trading hours. Some markets have a delay; please check your broker for true real-time quotes before making trading decisions.

I'm missing a stock — can you add it?

If a symbol you trade is not searchable, email us with the ticker and exchange. We continually expand our coverage and will do our best to include it in a future update.

Is CoinhakoBox free?

Please refer to the App Store listing for current pricing details. Any future subscription tiers or in-app purchases will be clearly disclosed before purchase.
